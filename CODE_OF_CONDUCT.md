# Code of conduct

EDDA is a small project run by volunteers for other players. The rules
are short.

## Be decent

Treat everyone in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews with
respect. Disagree about the code, not the person. Newcomers get patience;
questions that look obvious to you were not obvious once.

## No harassment

No insults, slurs, threats, sexual attention, sustained hostility or
pile-ons, in any channel that belongs to this project. This applies to
maintainers as much as anyone.

## No doxxing, in or out of game

Do not publish anyone's private information. In this project that
explicitly includes a commander's in-game location or movements: EDDA
exists on the promise that one player can never learn where another is
or has been, and revealing a commander's position, here or anywhere,
using anything EDDA produced, is off limits. So is sharing journals or
logs that name other players without their consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, and warn, block
or ban anyone who breaks these rules, at their discretion and without a
long process. Repeat or serious offences are a ban.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, `@terakilobyte`, on GitHub. If the problem is
inside a public thread, you can also flag it there and it will be
handled. Reports are treated in confidence.
