ELITE DANGEROUS: ANACONDA MK II UPDATE

Greetings Commanders,

The Anaconda Mk II Update is now live.

Faulcon DeLacy's legend has been reborn with the arrival of the Anaconda Mk II. A modern evolution of one of the galaxy's most iconic vessels. This next-generation ship retains the power, versatility, and commanding presence that made the original a favourite among Commanders, while introducing a all-new design and improved visibility.

Available now via ARX Early Access, alongside a range of fixes and improvements in this update.

Resolved an issue where Conflict Zones could fail to end as expected. (Issue ID: 85560) 
BUG FIXES:
Resolved an issue where on foot wing UI could sometimes fail to display correctly. 
Resolved an issue where the final wave of on foot AI were not spawning correctly during the Reclamation Point Operation. 
Resolved an issue where the New Starter Systems permit was being granted when ranking up in CQC.
Resolved an issue where Powerplay Modules could not unlock correctly when reaching the required rank. 
Resolved an issue where debug text could display when viewing Mission Giver details if their faction had the Feudal government type and the pending state of Retreat.
Resolved issues with Squadron Leader boards could not display the "Operations" category.
Resolved an issue where Planetary Mining resources were not being displayed in the Info Panel when scanning a planet with FSS. 
Resolved an issue where the destination lock for Planetary Ring Hotspots was no longer working.
Resolved an issue where text could overlap in the on foot mining UI when using the Rhino's mining rigs.
Resolved a disconnect caused by jettisoning cargo the player whilst collecting minerals from a Rhino mining rig.
Resolved an issue where vents on the side of dockable Megaships could visibly exhibit LoD transitions.
Resolved an issue where bloom could be too bright on certain objects.
Resolved an issue where light could bleed from the hangars on Megaships, resulting in it being visible around the landing pads. 
Adjustments made to spawn cupboards hitcheck to stop AI clipping through the cupboards doors.
Adjustments made to the lighting for the Rhino bundles in the Store previews. 
Resolved an issue with 'Edmund Mahon' Powerplay decals would drop below the intended level of detail. 
Adjustments made to various Powerplay decals to reduce clashing with various paintjobs.
Adjustments made to 'Archon Delaine' & 'Jerome Archer' Powerplay decals to improve quality.
